```Thou know’st not the weight of the hate...

That upon him I do now lay,
For though by landlord’s fate my mate,
His cursed damned game doth slay.
The peace I seek when night is deep,
Yet still his piercing voice doth leap,
Till slumber’s gate is barred to me
By all his hell-born devilry.```
